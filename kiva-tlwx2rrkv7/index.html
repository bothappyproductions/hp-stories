Your lenders already told you what keeps them lending. These people are filmed, cut and checked. Here is what they look like in the mail and in the inbox.

Direct mail

A postcard with a QR code

6 × 4.25 in, the USPS postcard size. One borrower per card, the lender's name merged in, and a QR code that opens that borrower's story. Flip it and scan the code with your phone: it works.

Donor email

A series that leads with a face

Three sends, each opening on a looping clip of a real borrower or lender. GIFs play in Gmail and Apple Mail; Outlook shows the first frame.
